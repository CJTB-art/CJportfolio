Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Pasa (“we”, “us”) is a study app for the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we collect

Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that provider. We never receive your Google or Facebook password. If your Facebook account has no verified email, we may not receive one.

Study data: your exam choice, study progress, scores, notes, bookmarks, flashcards, feedback and community posts you create, and the time you were last active.

How we use it

To sign you in, save your progress across devices, show your results and study plan, keep the service secure, and respond to your messages. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Cloudflare (hosting). They process data only to run Pasa.

Sharing

We share data only with these providers, or when the law requires it. Posts you make in community areas may be visible to other users.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data at any time. See how to delete your data.

Children

Pasa is meant for adult exam takers. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
