Delete Your Data

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Pasa account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to besinanchristejan@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Pasa data”. Send it from the email address on your account, or tell us the email you used to sign in (including if you signed in with Facebook or Google).

We will delete your account, profile and study data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Facebook users

You can also remove Pasa from your Facebook account under Settings → Apps and Websites. This stops Pasa from receiving new data from Facebook. To delete the data we already hold, use the email request above.
