01

LYR Workspace

2025 to 2026 · Built solo · Used company-wide

A calendar-centered workspace built on top of LYR's existing operations. It brings task planning and the company's connected tools into one place, with role-based accounts and an audit trail.

  • Flask
  • Calendar
  • Task planning
What's in it
  • Calendar with month, day, and week planning views.
  • A per-person To-do list for work without a time slot.
  • Plot tasks into a person's open calendar time.
  • Task assignments, reminders, subtasks, and audit history.
  • Connected operations tools behind role-based access.
What's changed
  • The dashboard was removed; the calendar is now the center of task planning.
  • Unscheduled tasks stay in To-do and can be plotted into available time.
  • Day and week views surface tasks still waiting to be scheduled.
02

PASA

2026 · Built solo · Interactive demo

A review app for the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET). Learners practice by subject (GenEd, ProfEd and Major), work toward a daily question goal, and track their readiness as they go.

  • SvelteKit
  • Interactive demo
What's in it
  • A baseline, topic practice, and timed subject practice.
  • Flashcards, an error log, and a weekly plan based on published questions and learner activity.
  • A practice signal based on section practice, the latest mock, and eligible flashcard recall.
  • Readiness is an activity signal, not a prediction of passing.
What's changed
  • The paper-inspired redesign now carries across public and learner-facing screens.
  • Routes, question content, scoring, and readiness behavior stayed the same through the redesign.
03

Milestone

2025 to 2026 · Built solo · React and Vite

A personal finance app I built to keep my own spending honest. It shows what's safe to spend until payday, splits each month's income across savings, investing, emergency and wants, and tracks bills, accounts and progress toward a savings goal.

  • React
  • Vite
  • Demo data
What's in it
  • Account balances and activity derived from a dated transaction ledger.
  • Safe-to-spend based on everyday account balances and unpaid bills before the next expected payday.
  • Income planning, bill tracking, cash-flow forecasts, savings goals, and spending insights.
  • Transfers, reconciliation, and undo for changes.
What's changed
  • Money movement is recorded as dated ledger entries; balances, totals, and activity follow those entries.
  • Payday-aware bill reserves feed the safe-to-spend estimate, and changes can be undone.
