01 / Work

Selected work

01

Branch stock cleanup and reporting

2025 to 2026 · Built solo · Multi-branch distributor, 19,000+ part numbers, about 25 locations

Branch inventory, sales and in-transit files came in with inconsistent branch names, the same part under several codes, and mixed formats. I built cleanup rules and monthly reports so purchasing could work from one reliable stock and sales picture.

  • Excel
  • pandas
  • Reporting
What I did
  • Standardized branch names across inventory, sales and in-transit files (39 different spellings mapped to 21 standard names)
  • Matched part numbers that exist under several codes (about 330 duplicate groups) so stock and sales roll up to one row
  • Classified stock into overstock, non-moving and depleting items using sales history, stock months and supplier lead times, to guide reorders
What changed
  • One standard month-end workbook now covers about 65,000 branch stock lines and 19,000+ part numbers
  • Duplicate part codes and mismatched branch names are caught before they split stock or sales across separate rows
  • Purchasing gets ranked overstock, non-moving and low-stock lists built from a full year of sales data (about 400,000 sales lines)
